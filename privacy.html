Privacy policy

DogScore Poster · last updated 13 November 2026

Who uses this app

DogScore Poster is a private tool run by the DogScore team on its own computer. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it connects to. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • Upload videos (YouTube upload scope): to publish videos the owner made to the owner's own channel.
  • Read the channel (YouTube read-only scope): to confirm which channel is connected and to read view, like and comment counts of the videos the app uploaded.

It does not read private messages, subscribers' personal information, or any channel the owner did not choose.

How the data is stored

Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are used only to make the requests above. Video view and like counts are kept on that computer to see which videos work best. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. DogScore Poster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions. Removing the account from the app deletes its stored tokens.
